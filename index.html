HOME INVENTORY FOR iOS

Know what you own. Find it when you need it.

Home Assets is a private, on-device home inventory app for recording assets, tracking warranties, scanning labels and exporting professional QR labels and reports.

No account required No analytics No tracking Data stays on-device

A dashboard built around your home.

See your inventory, value, warranty status, categories, rooms and recent additions at a glance.

At-a-glance statistics

  • Total assets, categories and total recorded value
  • Assets under warranty, expired warranties and priced assets
  • Personalised greeting based on the time of day

Interactive insights

  • Assets by Category donut chart
  • Assets by Room horizontal bar chart
  • Warranty Status proportional summary
  • Tappable chart rows for fast navigation

Recently added

Browse a paginated list of recently created assets with photo thumbnails, then tap any item to open its full details.

Record the details that actually matter.

Home Assets supports detailed asset records without forcing you to create an online account.

Identity and location

  • Name, category and room
  • Placement description and placement photo
  • Serial number entered manually or scanned
  • Main asset photo from camera or library

Purchase and warranty

  • Purchase date, vendor and price
  • Invoice number
  • Invoice image or PDF attachment with Pro
  • Warranty start and end dates
  • Notes for anything else you need

Search and filter

  • Search across name, placement, notes, vendor and invoice number
  • Filter by category, room, warranty, date and price
  • Removable active-filter chips

Manage in bulk

  • Newest-first asset list with thumbnails
  • Multi-select, Select All and bulk delete
  • Swipe to delete individual assets

Open any asset instantly with QR.

Every asset receives a unique QR code automatically.

Scan and open

  • Point the camera at a Home Assets QR label
  • Open the matching asset immediately
  • Haptic feedback confirms a successful scan

Four label layouts

  • QR Only
  • Text Below
  • Text Right
  • Text Left
  • Share an individual QR label as an image

Barcode scanner

Scan product barcodes directly into the Serial Number field. Supported formats include EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC-E, Code 39, Code 93, Code 128, PDF417, ITF-14, DataMatrix and QR.

Professional QR label export with Pro.

Prepare printable label sheets for home, office, storage or equipment tagging.

Flexible layouts

  • Filter by category and room
  • Four matching QR label styles
  • A4, US Letter and A5 page sizes
  • Automatic grid calculation and live preview

Printer-ready sizing

  • Custom dimensions in millimetres, inches or pixels
  • 72–300 DPI selection
  • Presets for Niimbot, Dymo, Brother, Zebra and generic labels
  • Export through Email, Share, AirDrop, Files or Print

Reports you can actually use.

Filter your inventory, choose the exact fields you need and export the results.

Detailed reports

  • Filter by category, room, warranty, date and price
  • Summary bar for asset count and total value
  • Warranty badges and purchase information

CSV export

  • Select from 13 exportable columns
  • Open in Excel, Numbers or another spreadsheet app
  • Send by email or share through the iOS share sheet

Made to fit your preferences.

Control how dates, values and QR labels appear throughout the App.

Categories and rooms

Add, rename or delete your own categories and room locations, with useful defaults included.

Regional formats

  • Choose between Jul 20, 2026 and 20 Jul 2026
  • Choose 1,000.00 or 1.000,00 decimal formatting
  • Choose from 20 supported currency symbols

Private by design.

All Home Assets data is stored on your device. It is never uploaded to the developer. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Photos are optimised locally before they are saved.
