01

Smart Dashboard

View asset totals, recorded value, warranty status, category breakdowns, room summaries and recent additions from one clear screen.

02

Detailed Asset Tracking

Keep photos, serial numbers, purchase details, placement, warranties, invoices and notes together for every asset.

03

QR & Barcode Tools

Generate a unique QR code for each asset, scan labels to open records, and capture supported product barcodes.

Private by Design

  • Export and share your information whenever you choose.
