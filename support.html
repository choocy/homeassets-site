Home Assets Support

Need help with the App, a purchase or your locally stored asset records? Start with the answers below or contact us by email.

Contact support

Email us with a clear description of the issue, your iOS version and the steps that caused the problem. Do not include passwords or payment-card details.

Email homeassets@icloud.com

Privacy and legal information

Review the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. The App also uses Apple’s Standard EULA unless a custom EULA is presented through the App Store.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my asset information stored?

In the current version, asset information is stored locally on your device. Home Assets does not currently provide its own account system or cloud synchronization.

Do I need to create an account?

No. The current version does not require a separate Home Assets username or password. Subscription eligibility is verified using Apple’s StoreKit system.

How many assets can I create for free?

The intended free version supports up to 10 assets. Home Assets Pro unlocks unlimited asset creation and any other premium features shown in the App.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the App’s Settings or subscription screen and choose Restore Purchases. Make sure the device is signed in to the same Apple Account used for the original purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, choose Subscriptions, select Home Assets and follow Apple’s cancellation options. Cancellation normally takes effect at the end of the current paid period.

Can you issue a refund?

App Store purchases and refund requests are handled by Apple under Apple’s policies. Use Apple’s official purchase-support process to request a refund where eligible.

What happens if Home Assets Pro expires?

The App will not intentionally delete your records merely because Pro expires. If you have more than the free limit, you may be prevented from adding new assets until you renew or reduce your asset count.

Will deleting the App delete my assets?

It may. The current version stores information locally, so deleting the App may remove its local data. Device backups or transfer tools may behave differently, but Home Assets does not currently promise cloud recovery. Keep independent copies of irreplaceable documents.

Why does the App need camera access?

Camera access is used to scan QR codes. Scanning is performed on the device and camera images are not sent to the developer.

Why does the App need photo access?

Photo access is used only when you choose to attach an image to an asset record. Select only the images you want the App to use.

Does Home Assets support iCloud sync?

Not in the current version described by these pages. If optional iCloud synchronization is introduced, the App and Privacy Policy will be updated.

How should I report a bug?

Email homeassets@icloud.com with the device model, iOS version, App version, what you expected, what happened and the steps needed to reproduce the issue. Screenshots are helpful when they do not contain sensitive information.
