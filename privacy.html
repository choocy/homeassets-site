Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Home Assets iOS application (the “App”) and this support website handle information. Home Assets is provided by an independent developer based in Malaysia (“we”, “us” or “our”).

Current version: The App does not require an account, does not provide cloud synchronization and stores your home-inventory content locally on your device.

1. Information you add to the App

The App lets you create and manage information about household assets. Depending on how you use it, this may include:

  • asset names, descriptions, categories and locations;
  • serial numbers, model numbers and QR identifiers;
  • purchase dates, prices, seller details and warranty information;
  • notes, tags and other information you choose to enter; and
  • photos or images you choose to attach.

In the current version, this content is stored locally on your device. We do not operate a server that receives or stores this content.

2. Camera access

The App may request access to your device camera so that you can scan QR codes. Camera images used for scanning are processed on your device and are not sent to us. You can control camera permission in iOS Settings.

3. Photo-library access

If you choose to attach an existing image to an asset, the App may ask you to select a photo using Apple’s system photo picker or request appropriate photo-library access. The App uses only the images you select for the purpose of maintaining your asset record. Those images remain stored with the App’s local data.

4. Subscriptions and purchases

Purchases and auto-renewable subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles payment credentials and billing. The App uses Apple’s StoreKit technology to determine whether an eligible purchase or subscription entitlement is active.

We do not receive your Apple Account password, full payment-card details or Apple Account email address through StoreKit. Apple’s handling of information is governed by Apple’s own privacy policies and terms.

5. Information we do not collect through the current App

The current App does not intentionally collect or transmit to us:

  • your name, email address or telephone number;
  • precise or approximate location;
  • contacts or address-book information;
  • advertising identifiers;
  • health or fitness information;
  • browsing history; or
  • your locally stored asset records.

6. Analytics, advertising and tracking

The current version does not include third-party advertising networks, cross-app tracking or third-party analytics services. If this changes, we will update this Privacy Policy and the App Store privacy disclosures before or when the updated practices take effect.

7. Support communications

When you voluntarily email us for support, we receive the information contained in your email, such as your email address, message and any attachments you choose to provide. We use that information to respond, investigate issues, prevent abuse and maintain reasonable support records.

Please do not send passwords, payment-card details or highly sensitive personal information in a support email.

8. This website and GitHub Pages

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub. GitHub may process technical information associated with website requests, such as IP addresses, device or browser information and server logs, in accordance with GitHub’s own terms and privacy practices. We do not use our own advertising cookies or analytics scripts on this website.

9. Data retention and deletion

You control the records stored in the App. You may edit or delete records within the App. Deleting the App may remove its locally stored data, subject to any device backup or operating-system behavior outside our control.

Support emails may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request, maintain support history, comply with legal obligations or resolve disputes. You may request deletion of a support conversation by contacting us, subject to any information we must retain for legitimate legal or security purposes.

10. Data security and backups

We design the App to use Apple platform security features, but no storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure or free from loss. Because the current version stores information locally and does not provide Home Assets cloud synchronization, you are responsible for maintaining suitable device backups. Do not rely on the App as the sole copy of irreplaceable receipts, photographs or warranty documents.

11. Children’s privacy

The App is a general home-inventory utility and is not directed specifically to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the current App. A parent or guardian who believes a child has sent personal information to our support email may contact us to request its deletion.

12. International use

The App may be available in multiple countries or regions. Support communications and website-related technical information may be processed in locations where Apple, GitHub or their service providers operate, subject to their respective privacy terms and applicable law.

13. Future features

We may introduce features such as optional iCloud synchronization, diagnostics, analytics or additional integrations in a future release. This policy describes the current version only. We will revise it when our information practices materially change and update the effective date above.

14. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the App, legal requirements or our practices. The latest version will be published on this page. Material changes may also be communicated within the App or through App Store release information where appropriate.

15. Contact

Questions or privacy requests may be sent to homeassets@icloud.com.
